# Texturas do terreno

Todas as texturas desta pasta vêm do [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) sob licença CC0 (domínio público),
redimensionadas e reempacotadas por `scripts/pack-terrain-textures.mjs`.

- **Grama** (`grass`): "Forest Ground 01" por Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/forrest_ground_01
- **Campo** (`meadow`): "Aerial Grass Rock" por Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock
- **Terra** (`dirt`): "Forest Ground 04" por Rob Tuytel, Rico Cilliers — https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04
- **Lama com musgo** (`mud`): "Brown Mud Leaves 01" por Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01
- **Rocha** (`rock`): "Rock Face" por Greg Zaal, Dario Barresi — https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face
- **Cascalho** (`gravel`): "Rocky Trail" por Amal Kumar — https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_trail
- **Areia** (`sand`): "Coast Sand 01" por Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01
- **Neve** (`snow`): "Snow 02" por Rob Tuytel — https://polyhaven.com/a/snow_02
